## How we work

1. **Start from your problem.** The first call is questions, not a pitch.
2. **A short written note before anything is built:** the first piece, what you will see when it is done, and what it should cost.
3. **A working piece every week,** with a written update whether or not there is good news.
4. **People approve what matters.** AI that touches money or a customer waits for a yes, and every action is logged.
5. **You own it.** Full code and documentation, so your team does not depend on us.
6. **A straight answer.** Including when the answer is that you do not need AI or automation for this.

**Engagement models:** hourly, fixed-price milestones, dedicated team or staff augmentation, and AI consulting.
